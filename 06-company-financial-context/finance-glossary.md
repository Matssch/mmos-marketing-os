# Finance glossary for marketers

- **GMV:** total gross value of completed transactions, not platform revenue.
- **Take rate:** percentage of eligible GMV earned by platform as fees.
- **Recognized revenue:** earned transaction fees and subscription service revenue under the modeled accounting assumptions.
- **MRR / ARR:** subscription run-rate indicators, not necessarily current-period recognized revenue or cash receipts.
- **Gross profit:** revenue less cost of revenue.
- **Contribution:** incremental profit remaining after costs relevant to a specific decision; specify included costs.
- **Marketing-only CAC:** marketing acquisition costs divided by acquired customers (not fully loaded).
- **Fully loaded CAC:** relevant marketing and sales acquisition costs divided by acquired customers.
- **Incrementality:** outcome caused by marketing activity, relative to a credible no-treatment counterfactual.
- **ROI:** define margin basis and denominator; revenue / media spend is not the same as profit ROI.
- **Operating profit:** gross profit minus operating expenses; excludes financing/tax in this simplified presentation.
- **P&L:** company income statement showing earned revenue, costs and profit over a period; not a cash-flow statement.
