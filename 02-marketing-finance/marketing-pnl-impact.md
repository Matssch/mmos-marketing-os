# Marketing impact on P&L

The objective is **financially accountable marketing**, not claiming CFO or company P&L responsibility.

| Commercial line | Marketing control | Financial interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing expense | Direct | Paid media, agencies, content, events, marketing tools and team budget |
| Qualified pipeline | Shared with sales | Forward-looking opportunities, not recognized revenue |
| Platform revenue | Indirect | Influenced through activation, transactions and subscriptions |
| Cost of revenue | Limited | More platform usage can create transaction and service-delivery costs |
| Gross profit | Indirect | Incremental revenue × incremental gross margin, subject to actual costs |
| Operating profit | Indirect | Incremental gross profit less additional marketing and other operating costs |

## Example: test investment

A hypothetical **additional** €100K campaign yields €180K genuinely incremental recognized platform revenue. At an assumed incremental gross margin of 75%, it generates €135K additional gross profit. Less €100K campaign spend = **€35K increase in operating profit** before extra sales, product or overhead costs.

This is a **scenario**, not a claimed result. If the campaign is funded by *reallocating* existing budget instead, we must compare its expected incremental contribution with the forgone performance of the displaced activity; treating the full €100K as new cost would then be wrong.

## Decision

Do not approve based on 35% alone. Require test design, customer-quality metrics, estimated operational cost and downside sensitivity. Revisit after observed cohort performance.
