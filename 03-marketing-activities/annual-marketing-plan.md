# Annual marketing activities and investment plan

**Role:** Simulated International Marketing Lead, MMOS Ltd.  
**Budget:** €2,000,000 annual plan; all values hypothetical.

| Activity | Annual budget | Primary goal | Lead KPI | Commercial KPI |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Paid search | €500,000 | Capture active demand | Qualified conversion rate | Incremental activated buyers |
| Paid social | €300,000 | Generate target-account demand | Cost per qualified account | Qualified pipeline accepted by sales |
| SEO and content | €250,000 | Compound non-paid discovery | Non-brand qualified sessions | Activated accounts from organic |
| Outbound / ABM | €350,000 | Reach priority accounts | Meetings with target accounts | New active supplier / buyer accounts |
| Events / partnerships | €250,000 | Develop ecosystem trust | Verified follow-up meetings | Qualified partner pipeline |
| Marketing technology | €150,000 | Improve measurement / lifecycle | Lead routing SLA and data completeness | Conversion / retention uplift |
| Agency / creative | €200,000 | Produce and optimize campaigns | Testing velocity | Incremental performance vs control |
| **Total** | **€2,000,000** | | | |

## Operating rhythm

- **Weekly:** pipeline quality, campaign pacing, landing-page conversion, sales response, test status.
- **Monthly:** budget variance, account activation, cohort quality, channel mix, modeled contribution and exceptions.
- **Quarterly:** reallocate across channels/markets based on measured incrementality, sales capacity, market readiness and expected margin.

## Three marketing programs

**Program A — Buyer acquisition:** high-intent paid search + procurement content → verified accounts → first transaction. Shared sales SLA and first-transaction onboarding sequence.

**Program B — Supplier growth:** ABM / outbound + partner marketing → qualified supplier discussions → verified inventory → premium-subscription trials.

**Program C — Activation and retention:** lifecycle email, relevant alerts, onboarding content, product education and CRO to improve activation and repeat usage.

## Guardrails

Do not optimize toward raw form fills. Deduplicate accounts, track qualification to activation, separate spend from sales capacity, and report pipeline as pipeline—not earned revenue. Every ROI claim needs attribution methodology and uncertainty.
