# Lifecycle marketing and conversion optimization

## Problem

Marketing performance should not stop at account creation. Dormant buyers and non-activated suppliers increase acquisition costs without generating sustainable commercial value.

## Experiments

| Test | Audience | Primary outcome | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simplified registration | New buyers | Verified-account conversion | Lower qualification quality |
| Supplier onboarding checklist | New suppliers | First inventory activation | Added support burden |
| Buyer alerts / nurture | Activated buyers | Repeat transaction rate | Unsubscribes and fatigue |
| Premium feature education | Active suppliers | Subscription conversion | Cannibalization of existing plan |

## Measurement

Use cohorts and test/control comparisons where possible. Report the **incremental** impact on activation, gross profit, and support cost, not only email open rates or form conversions. Coordinate implementation and event instrumentation with product and analytics teams.
