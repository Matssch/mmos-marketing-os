# Supplier acquisition and SaaS conversion program

**Audience:** distributors, wholesalers and verified inventory partners. **All business assumptions are simulated.**

## Hypothesis

A clear supplier ROI proposition plus fast onboarding will increase active inventory and premium subscription conversion.

## Activities

1. Segment target suppliers by inventory fit, region and company size.
2. ABM and LinkedIn outreach with commercial proof points.
3. Partner/webinar program on supplier visibility and lead management.
4. Joint marketing-sales qualification and onboarding checklist.
5. Premium tier trials with clear value demonstration.

## Funnel

Target supplier → meeting → qualified account → onboarded inventory → active account → premium subscription → renewal.

## KPI definitions

- Fully loaded supplier CAC: attributable marketing **and sales** acquisition expense ÷ newly acquired active suppliers.
- Marketing-only cost per acquired supplier: marketing acquisition spend ÷ newly acquired active suppliers.
- Subscription conversion: first-time premium accounts ÷ eligible activated supplier accounts.
- Retention and net revenue retention: cohort-based, not blended with new sign-ups.

Do not count buyer-side transaction commission again as supplier-side attributable revenue. Subscription revenue is counted once and recognized over service delivery periods.
