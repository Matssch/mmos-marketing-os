# Buyer acquisition program

**Audience:** procurement teams, retailers, and business technology buyers. **All figures and scenarios are simulated.**

## Hypothesis

Helping buyers verify product availability and compare qualified suppliers will improve the rate of first transactions among relevant target accounts.

## Activities

1. High-intent search campaigns segmented by sourcing use case.
2. Problem-led landing pages with account verification and transparent next steps.
3. Educational content for procurement, availability and transaction reliability.
4. Nurture sequence for registered buyers who have not completed a sourcing request.
5. CRO tests: form length, proof points, supplier availability and CTA language.

## Funnel and metrics

Qualified session → verified registration → activated sourcing request → completed transaction → repeat activity.

Measure account-level costs and conversion at each stage, not only form fills. Track paid and organic separately; connect activation to transaction fee revenue only when finance recognizes it.

## Pilot proposal

Test one market and customer segment with a documented pre/post-plus-control design where feasible. Primary decision metric: incremental gross profit net of incremental spend over a defined observation window. Secondary: activation rate, days to first transaction and repeat buyer rate.
