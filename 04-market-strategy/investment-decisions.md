# Marketing investment decision framework

For each market and activity:

1. **Business problem:** what commercial bottleneck are we solving?
2. **Proposed action:** specific audience, message, channel, cost, and owner.
3. **Economics:** marketing spend, sales effort, expected activation, incremental gross profit and timeframe.
4. **Measurement:** holdout, geo-test, randomized rollout, or a clearly limited attribution proxy.
5. **Constraints:** inventory, sales capacity, regulatory, payment and language considerations.
6. **Decision:** approve, revise, postpone, or stop, with measurable conditions.

### Example — simulated

A paid-search pilot appears to have low lead cost but poor buyer activation. Do not scale on CPL. Investigate qualification, landing-page expectations and first-transaction barriers. Shift budget only after testing activation and **incremental contribution**.
