# International market priorities

These market positions are **fictional planning scenarios**, not assertions about real conditions.

| Market | Simulated role | Marketing program | Main decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | Established | Lifecycle + conversion | Can activation improve without increasing spend? |
| Germany | Growth | Search + ABM | Is added acquisition investment profitable? |
| United Kingdom | Mature / competitive | CRO + content | Which channels deliver incremental contribution? |
| Spain | Developing | Localization + partner tests | What supports repeatable demand? |
| United Arab Emirates | Early-stage | Research + narrow pilot | Is market-entry investment justified? |

## Allocation approach

The workbook's marketing budget is first allocated by **activity**, not arbitrarily by country. Market-level investment is decided using opportunity size, readiness of the sales and supplier network, customer quality, expected contribution margin, and confidence in measurement.

## Market gate

Before expanding a program, require: credible target account list, local product fit, operational capacity, valid measurement, a test budget with stop rules, and a defined expected contribution-payback range. The UAE is one market among five, not a predetermined winner.
