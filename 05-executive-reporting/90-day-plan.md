# 90-day Marketing Lead plan

## Days 1–30 — Understand and establish baseline

Meet sales/product/finance leads; review customer journeys; audit tracking, CRM stages, channel spend, supplier capacity, and acquisition quality. Define common KPIs and budget reporting. Document data gaps, assumptions and bottlenecks.

**Deliverables:** funnel definitions, spend baseline, measurement specification, first prioritization memo.

## Days 31–60 — Launch controlled improvements

Fix priority tracking issues; rebuild top landing-page journey; align sales follow-up; launch a buyer activation test and supplier onboarding improvement; create monthly management reporting.

**Deliverables:** test briefs, campaign calendar, conversion scorecard, forecast vs budget.

## Days 61–90 — Decide and scale selectively

Assess observed test results and contribution economics. Stop weak activities, adjust investment where justified, publish a quarterly plan with accountable owners and decision gates.

**Deliverables:** results/limitations summary, recommendations, revised quarterly channel plan, company P&L impact scenario.

### Success criterion

Not 'more leads'. Success means better visibility into acquisition quality and a defensible case for where marketing investment should go next.
