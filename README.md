# MMOS Ltd. | Marketing Growth & P&L Impact

> An international B2B SaaS marketing leadership simulation: from marketing activities to commercial outcomes.

**Company:** MMOS Ltd. (fictional; *Mister Mats Operating Systems* is the inside joke)  
**Position simulated:** International Marketing Lead  
**Model:** B2B SaaS subscriptions with a transaction-enabled platform  
**Markets:** Netherlands, Germany, United Kingdom, Spain, United Arab Emirates  
**Status:** Portfolio simulation — all numbers, company information, and market positions are illustrative.

## Why I built this

Marketing is not only about leads, clicks, or campaigns. A marketing leader needs to decide where to invest, how to reach the right buyers, and whether those investments create commercial value. This project explores those decisions through an international B2B SaaS business.

My role in this **simulated case** is to own marketing planning and execution: positioning, demand generation, acquisition and retention programs, budget allocation, performance reporting, and recommendations to business leadership. I **do not claim to own the company P&L**. I use the full company P&L to understand the impact and limits of marketing decisions.

## The question

**How should an international Marketing Lead allocate a €2 million annual marketing budget across channels, customer journeys, and markets to grow qualified demand while improving contribution to company profitability?**

## What I own vs. influence

| Marketing Lead owns | Marketing Lead influences | Company leadership owns |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign plans, agency briefs, content, channel allocation | Qualified pipeline, new customer acquisition, platform activation | Pricing approval, product roadmap, company-wide costs |
| Marketing budget, tests, performance reports | Revenue, retention, contribution margin | Full P&L, cash flow, balance sheet |
| Marketing experimentation and optimization | Sales conversion with sales partnership | Overall operating profit and capital allocation |

## Marketing model at a glance

| Item | Simulated annual baseline |
|---|---:|
| Marketing budget | €2,000,000 |
| GMV (not revenue) | €120,000,000 |
| Marketplace fee revenue | €6,000,000 |
| Recognized SaaS subscription revenue | €1,800,000 |
| Company revenue | €7,800,000 |
| Gross profit (75% margin assumed) | €5,850,000 |
| Operating profit after €5M opex | €850,000 |

These are **company-level baseline assumptions**, not marketing-attributed results. The separate campaign scenario calculates *illustrative incremental contribution* and must not be mistaken for proven incrementality.

## How to use the workbook

Open [`mmos-marketing-finance.xlsx`](02-marketing-finance/mmos-marketing-finance.xlsx) in Excel. Change the assumptions and budgets, review the funnel, and evaluate the proposed pilot campaign. Read [the model guide](02-marketing-finance/model-guide.md) before quoting ROI figures.

## What this project does **not** prove

This is **not** a real company case, a record of actual campaign results, verified market research, or evidence that I have held full-company P&L accountability. It is a transparent scenario showing my decision framework and understanding of financial consequences.
