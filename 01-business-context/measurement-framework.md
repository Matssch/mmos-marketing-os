# Measurement and ownership

## Leading indicators

Target-account reach, qualified sessions, landing-page CVR, lead qualification rate, cost per qualified lead, supplier onboarding time, activation rate, sales follow-up SLA.

## Financial indicators

Spend vs budget, fully loaded marketing CAC, pipeline-to-revenue conversion, contribution profit from incremental campaigns, test ROI, subscription retention, gross profit and company operating profit context.

## Rules

1. State the numerator, denominator, time window, and attribution window for every KPI.
2. Separate **marketing-sourced**, **marketing-influenced**, and **experimentally incremental** outcomes.
3. Pipeline is **not** revenue. GMV is **not** platform revenue. ARR is **not** cash receipts.
4. Include sales costs in full acquisition cost when reporting full CAC; show marketing-only CAC separately.
5. Don't extrapolate a pilot's result to a full year without uncertainty and operational-capacity caveats.
6. Label simulated inputs as simulated, not external benchmarks.
