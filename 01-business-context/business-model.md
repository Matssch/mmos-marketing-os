# Business model and customer journeys

MMOS Ltd. is a **fictional B2B SaaS platform** connecting professional technology buyers with verified suppliers. Its core product is subscription software for procurement workflows, supplier management, product availability and transaction coordination. An optional transaction workflow produces platform commission income.

**Simulation only:** no company, customer, market, or product claims here are real.

## Core segments

| Segment | Job to be done | Key activation event | Business value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procurement teams / retailers | Source reliable inventory and manage supplier workflows | First verified sourcing request or transaction | Repeat transaction fees, potential buyer plan |
| Distributors / suppliers | Reach qualified buyers and manage inbound opportunities | Verified inventory + first qualified inquiry | Premium subscription, completed trade fees |

## Revenue streams

- **Subscription:** supplier seats/accounts pay a recurring fee for visibility, analytics and workflow features. A sample base case assumes 500 paying suppliers at €300/month for all 12 months, generating €1.8M recognized revenue.
- **Transaction-enabled workflow:** the platform charges an effective 5% fee on €120M simulated completed GMV, giving €6M recognized commission revenue, assuming agent accounting treatment.

GMV is never reported as company revenue. Subscription ARR is a run-rate metric; it equals recognized annual subscription revenue only in the static baseline.

## Marketing responsibilities

Marketing owns audience research, propositions, campaign messaging, SEO, paid media, outbound/ABM programs, nurture journeys, website CRO and channel-spend recommendations. Sales owns deal progression; product owns experience and roadmap; finance controls recognized revenue and official P&L.

## Funnel definitions

**Buyer:** target account → website/response → qualified lead → verified account → activated buyer → transaction → repeat activity.

**Supplier:** target account → response → sales-qualified supplier → onboarded supplier → active inventory → premium conversion → retention.

Track both funnels separately. Never add the same platform commission to both buyer and supplier attribution totals.
